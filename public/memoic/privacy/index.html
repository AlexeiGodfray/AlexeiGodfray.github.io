Memoic Privacy Policy

Effective October 26, 2026

Your notes and your privacy

Memoic is developed by Alexei Godfray. This policy describes the current version of Memoic, which stores your library locally on your device.

Memoic does not send your notes, recordings, imported documents, handwriting, or app usage to the developer. The developer does not operate accounts, advertising, analytics, or tracking services in Memoic. Websites and YouTube videos you choose to open are external services with their own data practices, described below.

What the app stores

Memoic saves the notes you create, ink strokes, typed text, selected photos, camera images, imported documents, audio and video, saved link URLs and optional titles, and related previews in its local app storage. It also saves drawing preferences and workspace settings so you can continue where you left off.

Memoic does not operate a cloud storage or synchronization service. iPadOS backups and device transfers may include app data, depending on your device and Apple account settings.

Photos, files, camera, and microphone

The photo and file pickers let you choose what to import. Memoic does not request access to your entire photo library. Your chosen photo or file provider may retrieve the selection from a service you use.

Camera permission is requested when you choose to take a photo for a note. Microphone permission is requested when you start an audio recording. You can use other note features without granting these permissions.

You can change camera and microphone permissions in iPad Settings. Revoking permission prevents future access; it does not delete photos or recordings already saved in your notes.

Document and handwriting features

Word documents are converted locally using bundled document-rendering libraries. This conversion uses a temporary, nonpersistent WebView with remote resources and network connections blocked.

Handwriting search and Copy as Text use Apple’s PencilKit recognition APIs. Memoic does not upload handwriting to a developer-operated server. Apple’s frameworks and system services are governed by Apple’s privacy practices and your device settings.

Websites and YouTube videos

Saving a link stores its URL and any title you enter locally. Memoic does not fetch link previews, website icons, YouTube thumbnails, or remote pages when you add a link or open a note. A website or YouTube player connects to the internet only when you explicitly open or play its card, or choose to open its URL in another app.

Live content loads directly from the website, YouTube, and the services they use. These providers may receive your IP address, browser and device information, the URL requested, and your interactions or viewing activity. They may use cookies or similar technologies, show advertising, and collect information under their own policies. Information you enter on a live page goes to that page's services. Memoic does not give them access to your local library or send the developer a copy of these interactions.

Memoic uses a nonpersistent WebView data store for live content, rather than a persistent browser profile. Closing the live content ends that WebView session. This does not prevent the website or YouTube from collecting information while connected or remove information they have already retained. Opening a link in another browser or app uses that destination's settings and data practices.

Memoic's YouTube playback uses YouTube API Services. By using this feature, you agree to be bound by YouTube's Terms of Service at https://www.youtube.com/t/terms. Google's Privacy Policy is available at https://policies.google.com/privacy. Other websites' privacy policies apply to their content and any services they embed. You can keep using your local notes without activating live content.

Clipboard, exports, and sharing

Memoic checks available clipboard types to offer paste actions. When you choose to copy or paste, the app uses the system clipboard for the content you select. Clipboard content may be available to other apps or through Apple’s Universal Clipboard, depending on your settings.

When you export or share, Memoic creates a temporary file and gives it to the app or destination you choose. That destination’s privacy and retention practices apply. Sharing does not give the developer a copy of your notes.

Exported PDFs show saved link cards and clickable URLs. They do not include an offline copy of a live website or a downloaded YouTube video.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your library stays in the app’s local storage until you permanently delete content or delete the app. Deleted notes, folders, and library videos move to Recently Deleted, where they and their app-owned attachments remain recoverable for 30 days. You can restore them or permanently delete them sooner from Recently Deleted in the library options menu. Expired items are removed when Memoic next checks Recently Deleted. Deleting content is not a guarantee of immediate secure erasure of every stored byte.

When you remove an object from a note, its media may remain available for undo, redo, or another copy in that note. Memoic cleans up media that is no longer needed at safe points. Temporary previews, import files, and exported files may remain until the app or iPadOS cleans them up.

To remove the app’s local data, use Delete App in iPad Settings; Offload App keeps its documents and data. Copies in device backups, the clipboard, original import locations, and apps or destinations you shared with are separate. Manage or delete those copies through the relevant device settings or service.

Contact and policy updates

For privacy questions, contact Alexei Godfray at godfrayalexei1@gmail.com. If you email, your email provider and the developer receive the information you choose to include so your request can be answered. This is separate from the app’s local library, which Memoic does not upload.

If Memoic’s data handling changes, this policy and its effective date will be updated.
