Create your first note

Memoic requires iPadOS 27 or later. Use Apple Pencil for handwriting, drawing, and lasso selection.

  1. In the library, tap + to open the new document menu.
  2. Choose Pages for a notebook, Vertical for continuous scrolling, or Spatial for an open canvas.
  3. Choose your paper style, enter an optional title, and tap Create Note.

Tap the narrow handle at the right edge to open drawing tools. The left handle opens stroke size and opacity. You can also open the handles with hover on a compatible Apple Pencil. When a tool button shows two stacked tools, tap it again while selected to switch between them.

Draw, scroll, and select

Apple Pencil draws; one finger scrolls. To select handwriting or images, choose Lasso or Rectangle Lasso, then use Apple Pencil to tap an image or draw around your selection. Finger drags over images scroll the note. The pen writes over images.

Use the selected object's controls to resize or rotate it. During rotation, the angle appears, and yellow guides show snaps at 0°, 90°, 180°, and 270°. Audio playback controls accept finger input; drag an audio pill's gray background away from the playback controls to move it.

Import documents and media

To open a PDF or Word document as a note, use Import in the library's + menu. Word import supports .docx files.

Inside a note, open the drawing tools and choose:

  • Add Photos or Videos to choose items from the system photo picker.
  • Take Photo to use the camera.
  • Import File to add a PDF, DOCX, audio, or video file.

If an import fails, make sure the file is fully available from its file provider. For audio and video, check that the original file plays on your iPad. Common supported formats include MP3, M4A, WAV, MP4, and MOV; the file must use a format your iPad can play.

Record audio and manage permissions

Choose Record Audio from the tools to add an audio pill, then tap its Record button. Tap Stop when finished and Play to listen.

Microphone access is requested when you start recording. Camera access is requested when you choose to take a photo. If you previously denied either permission, enable it for Memoic in iPad Settings and try again. Other note features work without camera or microphone permission.

The photo picker lets you choose individual items; Memoic does not request access to your entire photo library.

Saving and sharing your notes

Changes save automatically to Memoic's local storage on your iPad. There is no save button or account to set up. Memoic does not operate a cloud storage or synchronization service. iPadOS backups and device transfers may include app data, depending on your device and Apple account settings.

To share a note, open its three-dot menu and choose Share as PDF, then select a destination in the system share sheet. PDF exports are shareable documents, not restorable copies of your editable Memoic library or its playable audio and video.

If a save or import error appears, keep the note open and read the message before retrying. Deleting the app removes its local library, so avoid deleting and reinstalling it as a troubleshooting step when it contains notes you need.
